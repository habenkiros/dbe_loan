1. Purpose, Scope & Document Control

Purpose: Specify the AI-powered Credit Intelligence platform for DECSI — a digital credit factory for loan intake, document authentication, 7-step cashflow appraisal (MSME/corporate), GPS collateral valuation, multi-level committees, and post-approval disbursement tracking.

1.1 In scope

  • Staff web application (branch / district / HO roles)
  • Loan request lifecycle and role queues
  • Document checklist with OCR (English + Amharic) and authentication workflow
  • Appraisal Sheets 1–7 with Excel-faithful completeness gates, scorecard, analysis assist
  • Collateral catalog, field visit (online + offline PWA), coverage, lock/unlock, evidence pack
  • Configurable Branch → District → Head Office → Management committee voting
  • CBS customer number lookup / smart prefill (and optional transactions feed)
  • Digital Apply customer portal (applicant_portal) and staff online intake queue
  • Agentic Assist copilot and Credit Intelligence dashboards (hard rails — no auto-approve/disburse)
  • Post-approval closing pack, e-signatures, Finance disbursement queue, optional book ops
  • Seqela Market reporter portal (partners) for area price bands
  • MFA (TOTP), staff delegations, Risk Desk, on-prem Ed25519 license
  • Docker-based deployment on DECSI infrastructure (on-prem or private cloud)

1.2 Out of scope (unless separately contracted)

  • Full Temenos/CBS rip-and-replace or account booking engine
  • Full retail mobile/internet banking replacement (Digital Apply is loan self-service only)
  • General ledger / IFRS reporting suite
  • Hardware (tablets, GPS devices) procurement
Design principle: The Hub is DECSI’s credit factory. Core banking remains the system of record for accounts and fund disbursement.

5.1 Appraisal mode

Mode is driven by LoanCategory.appraisal_mode (msme / corporate) and stamped on LoanAppraisal. Corporate mode changes Sheet 1 KYC depth, Sheet 2 qualitative factor set (governance), and Sheet 3 financial statement emphasis while keeping the same 7-step wizard and committee factory.

8. Collateral, GPS & Offline PWA

8.1 Field visit

  • Wizard: Site → BOQ / valuation → Photos → Review
  • GPS accuracy capture; map pin (Gebeta or Leaflet/OSM)
  • Default policy examples: min 5 building / 3 land photos; weak accuracy threshold ~100 m; photo max distance from site ~200 m; declared-address mismatch flags
  • EXIF GPS vs live capture mismatch warnings; optional hard blocks
  • Submit locks dossier for audit; supervised unlock workflow

8.2 Offline Progressive Web App

ComponentBehaviour
Manifest / SWmanifest-collateral.webmanifest, collateral_sw.js
Local storeIndexedDB bundle for site data & photos
SyncPrepare offline → capture → Sync now when online
Secure contextOptional HTTPS nginx overlay for camera/GPS on LAN tablets
DECSI fit: Offline tablet path is required for rural branches with intermittent connectivity — core to DECSI’s field reality.

9. Committee & Disbursement Track

9.1 Committee engine

  • Configurable levels: Branch → District → Head Office → Management
  • Amount used for routing: Sheet 6 recommended amount (fallback: requested)
  • Member rules: by role scoped to branch/district/org, or named users
  • Thresholds: min approvals / declines per level (configurable)
  • Outcomes: Approve · Decline · Return to officer with notes
  • Read-only appraisal pack for voters (Sheets 1–7 summary)

9.2 Post-approval, closing pack & disbursement track

  • Conditions checklist clearance by officer/finance
  • Closing pack gates: collateral restriction, POA, title search, mortgage registration, notary/stamp verification
  • Digital agreement e-signatures (tablet pad; content-hash binding; borrower/officer/BM roles)
  • Schedule confirm → ready-to-disburse → Finance disbursement queue → disbursed flag
  • Optional partial disbursement and own-contribution verification

Fund release and account booking remain in CBS via operational handoff — the Hub does not replace the ledger.

11. Roles, Access Control & Security

Extended controls in the August 2026 release:

  • MFA — TOTP setup/verify; login lockout; security audit log export
  • Staff delegations — Admin-approved scoped cover (committee vote, appraisal, finance disbursement, assign officer)
  • Risk Desk — Pre-committee review queues; optional block on committee submit
  • On-prem license — Ed25519 SEQLA1 enforcement; no phone-home
  • Agentic Assist — Tool allow-list; full run audit; no approve/disburse/valuation tools

16. Acceptance Criteria & Test Approach

16.1 Functional acceptance (must pass)

  1. Create loan → upload required docs → OCR/auth statuses visible
  2. CBS customer # prefill populates empty Sheet 1 fields; conflicts Acceptable
  3. Complete MSME Sheets 1–7; qualitative <75% and E&S REJECT and DSCR<1.0 block finish as configured
  4. Corporate mode switches labels/factors for a corporate category loan
  5. Field visit captures GPS + min photos; offline capture syncs when online
  6. Coverage ratio appears on Sheet 5 / evidence pack
  7. Submit to committee; voter sees pack; Approve/Decline/Return works; routing follows recommended amount
  8. Role isolation: auditor/risk read paths; officers cannot cast HO votes without eligibility
  9. Digital Apply submission appears in staff online intake queue; customer sees Queue ID and status
  10. Closing pack gates block Finance disbursement until required legal steps cleared
  11. E-signature capture binds to agreement content hash
  12. Agentic Assist cannot invoke approve/disburse tools (verified in audit log)
  13. MFA required for configured roles; delegation cover works without password sharing

16.2 Non-functional acceptance

  • UAT environment restored from backup successfully (drill)
  • HTTPS field tablet can access camera/GPS (if HTTPS overlay used)
  • Production deploy runbook executed with DECSI IT sign-off

16.3 Test types

Unit tests (cashflow helpers, gates, modes) · Integration tests (CBS mock) · UAT scripts by role · Parallel-run sample files vs Excel baseline for credit sign-off.
