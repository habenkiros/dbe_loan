1. Purpose, Audience & The Ask

The ask of management: Endorse this financial proposal (total ETB 5,000,000 including VAT) and the companion technical proposal so Seqela and DECSI can proceed to formal documents and committee presentation — to license and deploy the new enhanced system network-wide.

Seqela developed the current live system for DECSI at no charge. What is in production today is queue management, user management, branch management, loan type management, and collateral type management — not the full credit factory. That queue system has already processed 53,901 applications and approved ETB 17.03 billion. The ETB 5 million is not payment for that past work. It is the price to deploy the new system: documents and OCR, 7-step MSME/corporate appraisal, GPS collateral, multi-level committees, Digital Apply, Seqela Market, Agentic Assist, Credit Intelligence, MFA, staff delegations, and post-approval through disbursement.

Why a separate financial proposal

DECSI requested a proposal and a financial proposal for management. Ethiopian committee evaluation typically requires: (1) a technical/business case, (2) a priced commercial offer with VAT, payment terms, and inclusions, and (3) a presentation pack. This document is item (2).

  • Institution-wide right to use the new Credit Intelligence platform for DECSI’s Ethiopian operations — including queue/admin already live plus the new modules in Section 1c.

  • Does not transfer Seqela intellectual property ownership; DECSI receives a perpetual usage license.

B. Implementation, configuration & rollout — ETB 1,250,000 including VAT

  • Requirements confirmation workshop (policy, products, hierarchy, committee thresholds, Digital Apply fee, KPIs).

  • UAT support, defect correction, and production hardening.

C. Training & change management — ETB 500,000 including VAT

  • Trainers-of-trainers for Credit, Branch, Engineering/Collateral, Finance, Risk, and IT.
  • Role-based sessions (loan officer, branch manager, committee voter, engineer, admin).
  • User manuals (staff, customer/digital apply, admin/IT) already prepared; updated to the accepted configuration.
  • Cascade pack so DECSI can train remaining branches internally.

D. Year-1 warranty & support — included

  • 12 months from formal acceptance (or network go-live of the first agreed wave, whichever is specified in the contract).
  • Bug fixes, security patches, and reasonable configuration support.
  • Named Seqela support channel; severity-based response as in Section 6.
  • Does not include work outside the agreed scope (Section 7) or DECSI infrastructure (servers, network, CBS vendor fees).

7. Out-of-Scope Work — By Agreement Only

Rule: Anything outside the agreed scope in this proposal is not included in the ETB 5,000,000. Such work is done only after Seqela and DECSI agree in writing on scope, price, and timing.

Examples of work that would require a separate written agreement (not an exhaustive list, and not pre-priced):

  • Deep CBS disbursement booking / automatic loan posting beyond customer-number lookup
  • A live NBE or credit-bureau connector beyond file-level flags already in the product
  • Additional on-site training waves beyond the agreed rollout plan
  • 24/7 support beyond the warranty / AMC response table
  • New product modules, channels, or integrations not listed in Sections 1c and 3
  • Data migration from unrelated legacy systems

No out-of-scope work starts, and no extra fee is due, until both parties have signed (or otherwise recorded) that agreement. The ETB 5,000,000 including VAT remains the complete price for the agreed scope.
