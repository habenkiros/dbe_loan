1. Purpose of this assessment

DECSI provided a 12-stage system process flow covering the loan journey from application to reporting and control. Seqela assessed the enhanced Credit Factory against that flow so management can see, stage by stage, what is ready for institution-wide rollout.

DECSI’s live production system today covers queue management, user management, branch management, loan type management, and collateral type management (53,901 applications processed on that platform). This report is not a claim that all twelve boxes are already live in every branch. It is an assessment of the enhanced factory built for DECSI — documents, appraisal, GPS collateral, committees, Digital Apply, post-approval, and disbursement — mapped to the process DECSI defined.

Headline. The Credit Factory is aligned with DECSI’s process. Nine stages are fully implemented. Disbursement to core banking is implemented in the product (Finance sees a booking preview) and is enabled when DECSI’s CBS connection is configured at go-live. Two items remain if the diagram is to be matched one-for-one: a dedicated legal administration queue after authorization, and a dedicated fraud detection product beyond the document, watchlist, and risk controls already in the system. This pack was re-assessed on 21 August 2026 after remote agreement signing, document-to-appraisal fill, and Finance booking preview were added.

2. How the assessment was done

  • Each box on DECSI’s system process flow was mapped to the corresponding Credit Factory module, screens, and controls.
  • Status is Ready when the stage exists end-to-end in the product; Ready at go-live when the product is built and a live DECSI connection (core banking) is required to operate it in production; Partial when the intent is covered in another step, not as the standalone stage drawn on the flow.
  • This is a product-and-process assessment for management and IT. It is not a priced proposal and it is not a branch UAT sign-off.
  • Quality gate: the Credit Factory automated test suite passed 276 of 276 tests on 21 August 2026 (including remote OTP signing, document extraction into appraisal, finance CBS preview, application create, and GPS/offline field retry).

3. DECSI’s 12-stage process

The assessment follows the process DECSI provided, in this order: Application → Documents → Queue number → GPS → Collateral estimation → Loan appraisal → Authorization → Legal administration → Processing workflow → Disbursement → Reports & analytics → Security & compliance.

What “Ready” means

The stage is implemented in the Credit Factory: staff (and, where relevant, the applicant) can complete the work on screen, with records, controls, and hand-off to the next stage.

What remains

Items that still differ from the drawing: legal work as its own queue after authorization; fraud as a dedicated product; and switching on the live core-banking booking at go-live.

6. Items to complete against the diagram

A. Legal administration as its own stage (box 8)

Today the factory will not disburse until government restriction, power of attorney, and related legal papers are uploaded and verified. If DECSI requires a Legal Administration desk between committee approval and Finance (legal officer role, legal queue, “cleared for disbursement”), Seqela will add that step so the screen path matches the drawing exactly.

B. Fraud detection as a dedicated product (box 12)

Role-based access, authorization levels, multi-factor login, password policy, browser security headers, and audit are ready. Document fraud controls (authenticity, identity match, suspicious files) and a watchlist are ready. If DECSI’s “Fraud Detection” means a full AML / investigation case system, that should be specified as a scoped addition. If document and origination controls are sufficient, box 12 can be treated as met for access and authorization, with fraud controls at intake.

C. Core banking at go-live (box 10)

Disbursement, Finance approval, and booking into core banking are built. At deployment, DECSI IT and Seqela enable the live CBS endpoints so disbursement posts to the book rather than a test connection. Customer lookup at application uses the same CBS party service when it is live.

7. Recommended next steps

Four decisions close the remaining gap between the Credit Factory and DECSI’s 12-stage drawing.

1. Confirm legal administration (box 8)

Keep legal papers as a disbursement gate (already built), or add a Legal Administration queue and legal officer role after authorization so the path matches the drawing one-for-one.

2. Confirm fraud detection (box 12)

Accept origination fraud controls (document authenticity, watchlist, risk desk), or commission a dedicated fraud / AML investigation product as a scoped addition.

3. Plan core-banking go-live (box 10)

Joint Seqela–DECSI IT session to connect customer lookup and disbursement booking to DECSI’s live core banking endpoints at deployment.

4. Walk the twelve stages (UAT)

A sample MSME file was walked through the running hub on 21 August 2026 (all 12 stages opened; 59 of 59 scripted checks passed). Repeat with Credit, Engineering, Cooperative, Finance, and IT on DECSI’s own environment as the go-live checklist.

For management. The enhanced Credit Factory is built to DECSI’s process and can be rolled out against this flow. The remaining decisions are process design (legal desk; fraud product) and the core-banking connection at go-live — not a missing origination, appraisal, GPS, or committee system.
