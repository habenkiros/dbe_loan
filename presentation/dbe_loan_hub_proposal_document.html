2. Why a Development Bank Is Not an MFI Origination App

The Cashflow-based MSME workbook DBE shared with banks and MFIs remains valuable — and it is already digitized as the 7-step wizard. It is the wrong headline for DBE itself.

DECSI (live factory)DBE (what iBOC encodes)
InstitutionMFI: MSME/corporate files, branch → district → HODFI: projects, lease assets, donor facilities, PFI books
DisbursementOne borrower, one (or few) releasesEquity first, then purpose-coded drawdowns over years
SecurityCollateral-heavy MSME (BOQ, land, movable)Viability first; first-degree security on the project; bank title on leased goods
After bookingWatchlist / collections case fileImplementation monitoring + named Project Rehabilitation

Operating rules the platform must encode (from DBE credit practice)

  • Priority sectors: commercial agriculture, agro-processing, manufacturing, mining/extractives (export-preferred), plus SME lease.
  • Debt–equity: typically 75:25 domestic new projects; 50:50 FDI / industrial-park; special ratios (e.g. 70:30 export linkage).
  • Large projects (≥ ~ETB 1bn): equity can be staggered (1/3, 2/3, 100% within 12 months of contract).
  • Tenor: medium 3–5 years; long up to 20 years including grace; grace up to 5 years (6 for tree fruit).
  • Lease: new capital goods; lessee capital band; ≥20% own contribution; ancillary ≤15%; contribution timing aligned with delivery.
  • IFB: Ijarah at branches (SME band); Murabaha project files at IFB Directorate / HO.
  • Idea: start-up ≤5 years, implement in Ethiopia, share ownership with DBE — quasi-equity, not an installment loan.
  • Pain already measured: industrial cycle time to first disbursement ~45 days. Follow-up and implementation delay drive poor recovery.
What not to sell as the headline: replacing core banking; seven independent systems; retail consumer as Wave 1 unless Credit confirms the book; greenfield. The line remains: extend the platform live at DECSI.

3. Proposed System — iBOC Architecture

Formal name: Development Bank of Ethiopia — Integrated Back Office Automation and Credit Management System. Short name: DBE iBOC. Cover line: Powered by Seqela AI Credit Intelligence.

Do not rebrand the live DECSI product. DECSI stays AI-Powered Credit Intelligence. Same codebase; DBE is a new institution configuration plus the product engines a DFI requires.

3.1 Design rule

One workflow engine. Each product family fills the appraisal slot and a few policy hooks (documents, conditions precedent, tranche rules, Sharia vs conventional schedule). Seven UIs that copy-paste the rest of the bank would be the failure mode.

Reuse (live at DECSI)

  • Customer, Digital Apply, OCR, document packs
  • 7-step MSME + corporate appraisal
  • GPS / BOQ / engineering QA
  • Risk desk, multi-level committees, MFA
  • Legal papers, e-sign, Finance queue, tranches
  • Watchlist, arrears, reschedule, write-off case file

Configure / build (DBE overlay)

  • Product families + engines (prototyped)
  • CRM / Appraisal / Engineering / Legal / Scan desks
  • Project NPV/IRR, equity, plant, visit-gated draws
  • Lease/Ijarah asset register; financed collateral kind
  • Wholesale PFI file; funding-window covenants
  • Rehab path; 45-day SLA; appeals

3.2 What “back office automation” means here

It does not mean a BPM tool that wraps Excel. It means: the 12 stages are the system of record, CBS remains the book, Credit Intelligence remains mandatory before committee on files that use that pack.

5.4 KYC and registration

Party type follows the family: person, promoter, or PFI institution on the same form. DBE product files run Scan/Admin plus parallel KYC on CRM, Engineering, and Legal. DECSI general files keep the existing document-auth path.

  • Implementation visits unlock the next purpose-coded tranche (civil, machinery, working capital, insurance).
  • Rehab: watchlist → restructure → technical assistance → recover → foreclosure last. Appeals up to President / Board.
  • SLA clock (informational, ~45 days): screening, technical, appraisal, review, committee, legal, first equity/loan release.

8.2 Hard policy gates (bank-configurable)

  • Qualitative <75% → hard block
  • E&S REJECT → hard block
  • Annual / stressed DSCR <1.0 → hard block
  • Bureau defaults → hard block option
  • Incomplete sheets 1–6 → cannot finish
  • Collateral coverage warning at policy minimum (default 1.0×)

8.3 Analysis Assist — decision support, not auto-approve

Surfaces weak/thin pillars, ask vs max cashflow capacity, bureau flags (thin file, defaults, score/band), corporate registration/audit flags, and hard blocks — so officers fix issues before committee waste.

Credit philosophy: AI-powered Credit Intelligence enforces standards and explains risk. Humans still decide. That is how you win both Risk and Business sponsors.

10. Approval Committee (live screenshots)

Problem solved: Incomplete packs, unclear routing, slow sequential sign-offs, and opaque returns. This section shows the actual committee workflow from the running system.

10.1 Officer submits to the approval committee

After Sheets 1–7 are finished, the loan officer submits the file to the configured Branch → District → Head Office → Management pathway, with optional notes for voters.

10.2 My approval queue — votes waiting for you

Committee members open a dedicated queue of loans requiring their vote at the current level — no chasing paper files across desks.

10.3 Cast your vote at the current level

Voters see overall status, current level, approval routing/pipeline, Sheet 6 recommendation summary, link to the full appraisal pack, and a vote form (Approve / Decline, amount supported, comments). They can also return to loan officer with required feedback.

Before voting, members open a read-only pack covering Sheets 1–7 — scorecard, cashflow/DSCR, E&S, collateral coverage, strengths/weaknesses — Print/PDF ready.
