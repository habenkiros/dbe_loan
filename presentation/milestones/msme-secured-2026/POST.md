## About Dedebit Microfinance (DECSI)

**Dedebit Credit & Saving Institution S.C. (DECSI)** — widely known as **Dedebit Microfinance** — is one of Ethiopia’s leading microfinance institutions, headquartered in **Mekelle, Tigray**.

- **Established:** 1993 (1986 E.C.) following a REST socio-economic study that identified lack of credit as a barrier to rehabilitation and development in Tigray.
- **Operational:** **8 March 1994**, first as a rural credit scheme under the Relief Society of Tigray (**REST**).
- **Legal form:** Reorganized as a **shareholding company in 1997** under Ethiopia’s microfinance legal framework (National Bank of Ethiopia).
- **Mission:** Provide savings and credit to rural and urban people traditionally underserved by conventional banks — farmers, women, youth, MSMEs, and other productive clients.
- **Credit focus:** Agriculture, industry/manufacturing, trade & services, construction, and **collateral-backed MSME facilities**.
- **Reputation:** Long counted among Africa’s larger MFIs by outreach; a cornerstone of inclusive finance in Tigray and beyond.

DECSI pairs outreach with institutional prudence — growth with collateral discipline.

Dedebit Credit & Saving Institution (DECSI) — Dedebit Microfinance — has reached a major milestone in **MSME secured lending**: collateral-backed credit that helps Ethiopian enterprises grow with discipline.

**By the numbers (as of today)**
- **ETB 29,562,221,974** requested  
- **ETB 17,025,387,000** approved  
- **25,397** approved customers  
- **53,901** total loan requests  

Behind every approval is a real business — a workshop adding capacity, a trader restocking inventory, a family enterprise creating jobs — financed against collateral that protects both the client relationship and the institution.

**About Dedebit Microfinance**  
Born from a REST rural credit scheme and operational since 8 March 1994 (share company since 1997), DECSI is headquartered in Mekelle, Tigray. For three decades it has delivered savings and credit to people and enterprises often left out of conventional banking — across agriculture, industry, services, and construction.

This milestone reflects DECSI’s credit teams, branch network, and the MSME clients who turn financing into productive enterprise.
