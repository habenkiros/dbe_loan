Product 02 · equb.seqela.com

Seqela Digital Equb — Tradition, Digitised

Modernises Ethiopian Equb while preserving trust, social structure, and flexibility — for individuals, businesses, associations, cooperatives, and banks.

How traditional Equb works Trusted groups · fixed rounds

Members contribute the same amount on a schedule. One member receives the full pool each round until everyone has been paid once. Notebooks, meetings, phone calls, pure trust.

Pain today What breaks at scale

Manual books · opacity · late payments · fraud & disputes · weak communication · geography limits · no reporting · cash handling risk.

Digital from anywhere

Create and run Equbs without paperwork logistics.

Transparent by design

Immutable trail of contributions, draws, and admin actions.

Still feels like Equb

Familiar rounds and social trust — not a foreign bank product.

Product 02 · six steps

How Seqela Digital Equb Works

01
Create an Equb
Name, category, amount, frequency (daily / weekly / bi-weekly / monthly), max members, dates, draw method, penalties, grace period, privacy, approval rules.
02
Invite members
Link, QR, phone invite, referral, public marketplace, or admin approval — then registration, KYC, phone verify, profile.
03
Collect contributions
Mobile banking, Telebirr, M-Pesa, bank transfer, wallet, or cash logged by admin. Auto: receipt, balances, reminders, missed-payment tracking.
04
Run the draw
Random lottery · sequential order · auction/bid · committee decision · priority (emergency, business need, seniority, history).
05
Disburse the pool
Wallet, bank account, cash, or institution rails — every transfer permanently recorded.
06
Repeat until complete
Continue until every member has received once — then close, renew, expand, or archive.

Product 02 · flexibility

Draw Models, Roles & Money Flows

DrawRandom lottery

System selects the recipient fairly and records the seed of the result.

DrawSequential order

Predefined member order — classic “who is next” discipline.

DrawAuction / bid

Members bid for earlier access to the pool.

DrawCommittee decision

Admins approve the round’s recipient under group rules.

DrawPriority rules

Emergency, business need, seniority, contribution history.

GovernanceGroup roles

Owner, chairperson, secretary, treasurer, auditors, multi-admins — role-based permissions.

Contributions can be

Fixed or variable · joining fee · membership fee · late penalties · early-payment rewards · standing instructions.

Pay-in rails

Bank transfer · mobile banking · Telebirr · M-Pesa · wallet · cash (admin-recorded) · payments auto-receipted and reconciled.

Product 06 · liabilities & engagement

PLSA — Prize-Linked Saving Account

Multi-behaviour loyalty that grows deposits, digital adoption, and cross-sell — principal never at risk.

Core model Campaign → Phase → Draw

National or segment campaigns with monthly, regional, and grand phases. Transparent, auditable draws.

Entries from behaviour Save · Pay · Repay · Refer

Tickets from deposits, digital channel use, timely loan repayment, referrals — rules configured per campaign.

Institution Marketing-funded prizes

Prize budget from institutional marketing — never deducted from customer deposits. Interest rules remain bank policy.

Why Product & Innovation teams care

Launch savings, women, youth, harvest, or scholarship campaigns without rebuilding CBS. Measure digital channel lift and multi-product ownership.

Integration principle

Service layer on CBS + mobile channels. Does not replace core banking — attributes and motivates journeys you already run.
