Four clear steps

No branch visit just to start. Finish at your pace; pick up with staff using your queue ID.

  1. Register once

    Use your mobile number and DECSI customer number so we can match your core-banking profile when available.

  2. Choose your loan

    Select product, preferred branch, amount, and purpose — then upload only the documents for that loan type.

  3. Pay the fee

    Complete the processing fee online through a secure payment partner. You will get confirmation before submit.

  4. Track status

    Submit to receive a queue ID. Check progress any time from My applications on phone or desktop.

What you will need

Have these ready before you begin so your application moves smoothly.

  • 1

    Customer number

    Your DECSI customer number from the branch or passbook — used to look up your registered name and phone.

  • 2

    Active mobile

    A working phone number you can access for sign-in, password recovery, and status updates.

  • 3

    Required documents

    ID and product-specific files (clear photos or PDFs). The form lists exactly what each loan type needs.

  • 4

    Payment method

    Ability to pay the listed processing fee online when you reach the fee step.

Branch when needed
