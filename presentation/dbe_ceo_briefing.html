03 · Partnership with DBE

Operating model

DBE owns the methodology. Seqela runs the digital factory.

DBE

Champion & standard owner

Cashflow MSME Excel, wholesale lines, TA, policy thresholds. Endorse, co-brand, optionally use internally. Core banking stays with DBE.

Seqela

Technology operator

Host or private deploy. Configure products, gates, hierarchy. Train credit and field teams. CBS customer prefill. Keep sheets Excel-faithful as DBE updates them.

Banks & MFIs

Adopting PFIs

Pilot selected branches and products. Align committees to policy. Measure cycle time and file quality. Scale after the pilot.

01

Recommend

Letter / circular that the digital factory is the preferred implementation of DBE’s cashflow workbook.

02

Co-brand

“DBE methodology · Seqela platform” for PFI buy-in and donor visibility.

03

Bundle with TA

Configuration and training ride EU/KfW technical assistance already in the project.

04

Afford

Shared instance for small MFIs; dedicated / private deploy for large banks and DBE.
