4. Gaps that keep the flow from 100%

8. Legal administration is a finance gate, not a module · 68%

The diagram places prohibition of collateral after authorization as its own step. The product stores restriction, power of attorney, title search, mortgage registration, and notary papers on the post-approval screen and blocks mark-disbursed until they are verified. There is no lawyer role, legal queue, or legal_cleared status. Closing the gap means a Legal Administration queue between committee_approved and Finance.

12. Fraud/AML is origination monitoring, not goAML · 90%

A dedicated Fraud & AML desk now opens investigation cases from duplicate/suspicious documents and banking-history anomaly scores. Open cases can block committee submit and disbursement. Residual gap vs a bank AML product: no live CBS transaction stream, no device/velocity engine, and no regulatory SAR/goAML filing.

10. Core banking disbursement is mock until DECSI_BASE_URL · 85%

The path post-approval → Finance approval → officer confirm → CBS book is fully coded (finance_ready_to_book, cbs_client.py, tests). Finance now sees a CBS payload preview and is blocked without a customer number. Default settings use DECSI_CBS_USE_MOCK_LEDGER=True. Live Temenos booking requires DECSI_BASE_URL. Customer lookup refuses silent mock when a live URL is set.

Adjacent: Seqela Market staff admin is unwired · portal only

The external market portal at /market-portal/ is live. Staff market admin views exist under partners/ but are not mounted in the root URL conf (tests expect /partners/ to 404). This is outside the 12 boxes but is part of the surrounding product.
