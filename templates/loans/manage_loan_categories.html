Manage Loan Categories

Each loan type picks a product family. The family default fills appraisal method and collateral; you can change them, or turn collateral off for unsecured products.

  • Project financing — promoter, debt–equity, equity before first release
  • Lease / Ijarah — new asset, 20% contribution, bank holds title
  • Wholesale / PFI — institution file, not MSME cashflow (usually no collateral)
  • Murabaha — cost-plus sale (TA’AWUN)
  • Idea — quasi-equity, DBE share, start-up ≤ 5 years (usually no collateral)
  • External fund — same credit file, tagged to a donor window
