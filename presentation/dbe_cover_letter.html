Re: Partnership proposal — digitizing DBE’s Cashflow-based MSME loan appraisal Excel into a shared platform for banks and microfinance institutions

Dear Sir/Madam,

On behalf of Seqela, we are pleased to submit this partnership proposal to the Development Bank of Ethiopia. DBE prepared and shared the Cashflow-based MSME loan appraisal Excel workbook so banks and MFIs could appraise MSME projects on cashflow capacity — not collateral alone. That methodology is sound. Adoption at scale is still blocked by spreadsheet variance, version drift, and weak operational workflow.

Seqela has fully digitized that workbook into a working AI-powered Credit Intelligence platform — seven guided sheets, DSCR and stress gates, bilingual document authentication, GPS collateral evidence, multi-level committees, Digital Apply, Seqela Market, Agentic Assist, Credit Intelligence, MFA, delegations, closing pack with e-signatures, and post-approval through Finance disbursement — proven in live Ethiopian MFI operations.

We respectfully request DBE’s partnership to champion sector adoption and help make the platform affordable for commercial banks and microfinance institutions — so the methodology DBE promoted becomes standard practice, not a file on a desktop.

We are ready to present a live demonstration to Credit, MSME/lease financing, IT, and partnership leadership, and to design a co-branded rollout and affordability model with DBE.

Thank you for your time and for DBE’s leadership in cashflow-based MSME finance.

Yours sincerely,
