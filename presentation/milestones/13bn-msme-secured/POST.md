## LinkedIn / Facebook post (English)

**ETB 13 billion.**

That is how much Dedebit Credit and Savings Institution (DECSI) has disbursed in MSME secured loans — credit given against collateral, so Ethiopian micro, small, and medium enterprises can invest with confidence and repay with discipline.

Behind every birr is a real business: a workshop expanding capacity, a trader restocking inventory, a family enterprise turning opportunity into employment.

Secured MSME lending is how DECSI pairs growth with prudence — collateral that protects the institution, and capital that unlocks the next chapter for entrepreneurs across its network.

This milestone is a testament to DECSI’s credit teams, branch network, and the MSME clients who turn financing into productive enterprise.

**ETB 13 billion disbursed. Collateral-backed. Built for MSMEs.**
