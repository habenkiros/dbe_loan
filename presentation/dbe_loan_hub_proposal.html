Lease and Ijarah Share the Asset — Not the Contract Math

Conventional lease (hire-purchase)

  • New capital goods only
  • Supplier, serial, commissioning
  • Lessee contribution (policy ≥20%)
  • Ancillary (insurance, transport, install) capped
  • Bank holds title until last installment
  • GPS on the asset location
AND

Ijarah (TA’AWUN)

  • Same asset / supplier register
  • Rental, not interest schedule
  • Sharia board review log
  • Bank as lessor until transfer
  • Insurance with DBE as co-beneficiary
  • SME Ijarah at branches; Murabaha projects at IFB / HO

Murabaha is a separate engine: cost-plus on financed goods. IFB is not a conventional loan with the interest label removed.

Wholesale and External Fund Are Two Different Questions

Wholesale engine Can this PFI manage and repay a facility?

Institution profile, capital, liquidity, NPL/PAR, governance, existing DBE exposure, on-lending strategy, sector/geo limits. No physical collateral — the book is the security.

Fund overlay Which donor line, and are covenants held?

KfW/EU 218.26, RUFIP III, SMEFP as funding windows. Envelope, 4.5% / ≤11%, 30% women / 20% youth, PAR90, eligible regions — tagged on any product family.

SMEFP already splits lease retail vs WC wholesale. Pretending they are one object would fail donor audit.
