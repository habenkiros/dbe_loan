1. Executive Summary & The Ask to DBE

The ask: Partner with Seqela to endorse, co-brand, and roll out a digital platform that faithfully automates DBE’s Cashflow-based MSME loan appraisal Excel — and help structure an affordable path so commercial banks and microfinance institutions can adopt it at scale.

DBE prepared the Excel workbook and shared it so institutions financing MSME projects could underwrite on cashflow capacity, DSCR, character, E&S, and collateral coverage — not informal judgment or collateral alone. That was the right national standard.

The remaining gap is operational: Excel files fork by branch, formulas change quietly, officers re-key data, committees receive incomplete packs, and smaller MFIs cannot afford to build their own systems. Seqela has already closed that gap with a live platform used in Ethiopian MFI credit operations.

2. DBE’s Excel — Intent vs Adoption Reality

The Cashflow-based MSME loan appraisal workbook was designed to give banks and MFIs a common, rigorous way to assess MSME projects — especially where physical collateral is thin and repayment capacity must be proven.

What DBE shared (intent)

  • Standard MSME cashflow appraisal language
  • Character / qualitative factors
  • Cashflow, DSCR, stress testing
  • E&S screening
  • Collateral worksheet & decision summary
  • Shared freely / promoted to FIs

What still happens in practice

  • Multiple Excel versions across branches
  • Formula edits and local “shortcuts”
  • No enforced completeness gates
  • Weak document & GPS evidence linkage
  • Committee packs assembled manually
  • Smaller MFIs struggle with capacity & cost

Why this matters for DBE now

  • DBE finances and enables MSME ecosystems through project finance, lease finance, and wholesale channels to banks/MFIs.
  • If partner FIs underwrite inconsistently, portfolio quality and development outcomes suffer upstream.
  • Digitizing the methodology DBE already authored is the highest-leverage way to raise standards sector-wide.
  • Affordability matters: without a shared platform model, only large banks can build; MFIs stay on fragile spreadsheets.
Strategic framing: DBE owns the methodology. Seqela operates the digital factory. Banks and MFIs get a standard they can afford. MSME borrowers get faster, fairer, cashflow-based decisions.

  • File sits waiting for physical sign-offs
  • Committee gets incomplete packs

With Seqela’s digital factory (DBE-faithful)

  • GPS-attested visits; evidence pack for voters

Target cycle: days, not weeks

  6. Pre-committee — Operations and finance manager queues
  7. Committee — Amount-routed multi-level vote; pack; return option

Application → documents → collateral → appraisal → authorization → disbursement.

8. Module C — Scorecard, Gates & Analysis Assist

Problem solved: Subjective “gut feel” decisions and files that reach committee before policy is met.

8.1 Explainable 4-pillar score (/100)

PillarWeightWhat it measures
Qualitative / character25Sheet 2 business & character quality
Financial / cashflow35DSCR, stress DSCR, capacity vs ask, ratios
Environmental & social15Risk category & eligibility outcome
Collateral coverage25Coverage vs ask (e.g. ≥1.5× full points)

Bands: Strong ≥80 · Acceptable ≥60 · Weak ≥40 · Unacceptable <40.

8.2 Hard policy gates (bank-configurable)

  • Qualitative <75% → hard block
  • E&S REJECT → hard block
  • Annual / stressed DSCR <1.0 → hard block
  • Bureau defaults → hard block option
  • Incomplete sheets 1–6 → cannot finish
  • Collateral coverage warning at policy minimum (default 1.0×)

8.3 Analysis Assist — decision support, not auto-approve

Surfaces weak/thin pillars, ask vs max cashflow capacity, bureau flags (thin file, defaults, score/band), corporate registration/audit flags, and hard blocks — so officers fix issues before committee waste.

Credit philosophy: AI-powered Credit Intelligence enforces standards and explains risk. Humans still decide. That is how you win both Risk and Business sponsors.

9. Module D — GPS Collateral & Evidence Packs

Problem solved: WhatsApp photo chaos, unclear site presence, disputed coverage, weak dual control.

9.1 Field visit with offline sync & map verification

  • Building BOQ (construction catalog × woreda unit prices), land, other/movable
  • GPS site mark with accuracy; EXIF vs live GPS mismatch warnings
  • Min photos (e.g. 5 building / 3 land); photo distance policy (default 200m)
  • Offline tablet PWA: prepare offline → capture → Sync now
  • Lock after submit; supervised unlock; engineering QA pipeline

9.2 Collateral evidence pack for committee & audit

One dossier: coverage vs ask, policy checks, BOQ summary, GPS-tagged photos, map, and recent audit events — with Print/PDF, Export JSON, Export ZIP.

9.3 Policy configuration

Bank risk teams tune min photos, GPS accuracy thresholds, coverage minimums, and distance flags — without code changes.

Bank benefit: Committee-ready proof; dual control; rural-ready offline capture; reconstructable audit.

10. Module E — Approval Committee (live screenshots)

Problem solved: Incomplete packs, unclear routing, slow sequential sign-offs, and opaque returns. This section shows the actual committee workflow from the running system.

10.1 Officer submits to the approval committee

After Sheets 1–7 are finished, the loan officer submits the file to the configured Branch → District → Head Office → Management pathway, with optional notes for voters.

10.2 My approval queue — votes waiting for you

Committee members open a dedicated queue of loans requiring their vote at the current level — no chasing paper files across desks.

10.3 Cast your vote at the current level

Voters see overall status, current level, approval routing/pipeline, Sheet 6 recommendation summary, link to the full appraisal pack, and a vote form (Approve / Decline, amount supported, comments). They can also return to loan officer with required feedback.

10.4 One-screen committee appraisal pack

Before voting, members open a read-only pack covering Sheets 1–7 — scorecard, cashflow/DSCR, E&S, collateral coverage, strengths/weaknesses — Print/PDF ready.

13. Affordability Path for Banks & MFIs

The Excel was shared to raise standards. Software must not recreate a new exclusion — where only large banks can digitize.

Design principles

  • One core product — configure, don’t rebuild
  • Tiered packages by FI size (MFI / mid bank / large bank)
  • Shared cloud or private deploy options
  • Pilot-first commercials (fixed scope)
  • Training included in implementation

How DBE makes it affordable

  • Volume commitment across FI cohort → lower unit price
  • Co-fund TA / training from development programs
  • Prefer shared multi-tenant for small MFIs
  • Phased modules (appraisal-first, then collateral/committee)
  • Transparent SLA & support for rural branches
