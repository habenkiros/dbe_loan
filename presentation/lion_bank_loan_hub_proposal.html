Feature deep-dive · 01

Documents That Defend the Bank

Stop incomplete and suspicious files before appraisal burns officer time.

Bilingual OCR (English + Amharic)

PDFs and images extracted with Tesseract eng+amh — critical for Ethiopian KYC packs, licenses, and IDs.

Identity field matching

Compares OCR text to applicant name, phone, TIN (and optional business name). Weighted score; strict mode can block upload.

Integrity automation

File type/MIME checks, size limits, SHA-256 duplicate detection, content keyword validation — less “trust the attachment.”

Clear auth statuses

Pending → Auto-passed → Needs manual review → Verified authentic → Rejected. Verdicts: Authentic / Suspicious / Not authentic / Inconclusive.

→
Problem solved

Customers bouncing between branch visits; officers wasting days on incomplete packs; weak identity assurance.

✓
Bank benefit

Higher first-time completeness (target 95%+), fewer fraud gaps, cleaner files entering credit analysis.

Feature deep-dive · 03

Explainable Scorecard + Hard Policy Gates

4-pillar score (/100)

Qualitative — 25

Character & business quality from Sheet 2.

Financial — 35

DSCR, stress, capacity vs ask, ratios.

E&S — 15

Risk category & eligibility outcome.

Collateral — 25

Coverage vs ask (e.g. ≥1.5× full points).

Bands: Strong ≥80 · Acceptable ≥60 · Weak ≥40 · Unacceptable <40

Gates that protect portfolio quality

  • Qualitative <75% → hard block (configurable)
  • E&S REJECT → hard block
  • Annual / stressed DSCR <1.0 → hard block
  • Bureau defaults → hard block option
  • Incomplete sheets 1–6 → cannot finish
  • Collateral coverage warn at policy min (default 1.0×)
Analysis Assist (not auto-approve)

Surfaces weak pillars, ask vs capacity, bureau flags, and hard blocks — so officers fix issues before committee waste.

Feature deep-dive · 04

GPS-Governed Collateral That Committees Trust

Three asset classes

Buildings (construction BOQ × woreda unit prices), land (m² × unit price), other/movable with required photo types.

Field GPS rules

Min photos (e.g. 5 building / 3 land); weak GPS >100m needs attestation; photo distance & EXIF mismatch warnings.

Offline tablet PWA

Work without signal; sync site GPS, photos, BOQ later — built for rural and weak-connectivity branches.

Lock & unlock

Submit locks valuation; corrections need supervised unlock request — no silent edits after the fact.

Engineering QA

Pipeline: assigned → in progress → submitted → pending eng QA → approved or returned for correction.

Evidence pack

Totals, coverage, readiness, maps, photos, last 100 audit events — exportable for committee & audit.

✓
Problem → benefit

Ends WhatsApp photo chaos and “was the officer really at the site?” debates. Committee sees verified coverage and location proof in one pack.

Feature deep-dive · 05

Committees That Decide Faster — With Better Packs

Amount-routed levels (configurable)

Default model — mapped to Lion Bank hierarchy:

  • Branch — officer, branch manager, accountant
  • District — district manager + peers
  • Head office — ops, finance, credit committee
  • Management — CEO / VP / board as configured

Min votes to approve or decline per level (default 2). Levels can be skipped by amount band with explicit reason.

One-screen committee pack

Read-only, print/PDF-ready:

  • Sheets 1–7 summary
  • 4-pillar scorecard & credit history
  • E&S risk + eligibility
  • Collateral totals & coverage
  • Risks, conditions, amortization
  • Link to GPS evidence pack

Outcomes: Approve · Decline · Return to officer with feedback for rework.

No more incomplete paper packs. Voters see the same truth. Returns create a coaching loop, not a black hole.
